### [Slide 45] Deep-Dive: Privacy & Security - Automatic Content Recognition (ACR)
- **SubTitle**: Digital Fingerprinting Without Transmitting Original Audio/Video, Strict Ad Separation
- **PPT 원본 노트 발췌**:
  > Let us now move on to Automatic Content Recognition, or ACR. ACR is a technology that helps a TV identify what content is being viewed. Because ACR operates at the TV level, it can recognize content across supported sources, rather than being limited to the usage information available within a single application. This broader understanding of viewing activity can support content-related experiences such as personalized content recommendations, audience segmentation, viewing and audience trend analysis, and other content-aware services. A common misconception is that content recognition requires the original video or audio to be recorded and sent to a server. In reality, ACR identifies content by matching digital fingerprints extracted from audio signals. The original video and audio are not transmitted or stored for content-recognition purposes. This slide highlights three important principles. First, ACR uses digital fingerprints rather than original content. Second, ACR operates only when the required conditions are met and the customer has accepted the Viewing Information Agreement. Third, ACR consent and interest-based advertising consent are separate customer choices. Agreeing to ACR does not automatically allow the information to be used for interest-based advertising
